# Job Evaluation Framework

## Core Skill Match Areas
- **Strong Match:** Android Development (Kotlin, Java, Jetpack Compose, KMP), Mobile Architecture, Gradle Build Systems, DevEx Automation (Bash), Technical Leadership.
- **Moderate Match:** Full-Stack/Backend (React Native, Firebase, GCP), Web Development (JS/TS), Security/Authentication Integrations.
- **Weak Match:** Roles exclusively focused on iOS (Swift/Objective-C), embedded systems, or legacy data engineering.

## Career Goals & Motivation Filters
- **Ideal Role:** Principal Android Engineer, Staff Mobile Engineer, or Mobile Architect focusing on large-scale platforms, developer experience (DevEx), and technical strategy.
- **Target Environments:** FinTech/FinServ, Organizations undergoing modernization, and Non-Tech sectors where technical leadership can drive significant business value and "stand out."
- **Key Motivators:** Reducing technical debt, standardizing architecture, mentoring engineering teams, and improving developer tooling/workflows.
- **Location & Salary:** 
  - **Ideal:** Remote or Pittsburgh-based.
  - **Acceptable (Super-commute):** New York City (NYC) or Washington D.C. (DC) for $200k+ roles.
  - **Borderline:** San Francisco (SF) or Los Angeles (LA) relocation if compensation maintains purchasing power parity ($200k+).
  - **Stretch:** Philadelphia.
- **Deal-breakers:** Consulting roles, hybrid application tech stacks (React Native/Flutter) as the primary focus, lack of architectural agency, highly siloed environments, and pure People Management roles (target is Technical Leadership).

## Calibration from Past Applications
- **High-Comp Relocation Signal:** High-comp roles in SF/NYC (OpenAI, Disney) are strong fits despite relocation requirements, provided base compensation clears the $200k threshold to maintain purchasing power parity with Pittsburgh.
- **Sweet Spot:** Roles with "Staff," "Principal," or "Lead Architect" titles (The Trade Desk, Robinhood, Waymo, OpenAI) consistently score 90+ based on the requirement for systemic impact and technical strategy.
- **Exclusion Signal:** Be cautious with "Founding" or "Full Stack" roles where iOS or deep non-GCP/Firebase backend is a primary requirement (e.g., SentiLink rejection).
- **Conversion Reality Check:** Be wary of high fit scores; as of Oct 2026, very few applications have moved to interview stages despite strong technical alignment. Scoring should prioritize "Grounding" and "Surgical Fit" over broad technical matches.
